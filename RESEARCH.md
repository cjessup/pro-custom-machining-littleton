# Pro Custom Machining — research notes

Demo redesign for the live site at https://www.procustommachining.com/. The business is Pro Custom Machining, Inc., a family-owned custom CNC machine shop at 9903 Titan Ct #9, Littleton, CO 80125. The published line of work is precision CNC machining: CNC milling and turning, laser engraving, automatic screw machine services, custom prototype and production parts, product development, component assembly, custom installation services, and plating, anodizing, and other finishing services. Equipment they list: CAD/CAM software, 6 CNC vertical machining centers, 4 CNC turning centers, a 50 watt fiber laser, 4 automatic screw machines, manual mills and lathes, and QC inspection and support equipment.

## Original site

Fetched home, services, about, contact, and gallery (the only pages in the nav).

- Stack: Turbify / Yahoo SiteBuilder, theme “streamline banner palette1” (`theme.css`). Page locked at 1050px. No viewport meta. Headlines set in Impact; body in Helvetica.
- Palette taken from that theme and the logo, not invented: accent `#ff6f20`, near-black `#191919`, cream page `#f1f0e5`, text gray `#646464`, white. The logo is a black starburst with a thin orange ray and “PROCUSTOMMACHINING.COM”.
- Title and description on Home and Services still contain leftover Pinterest “SaveSave” artifacts. Those were dropped. The orange “Save” buttons are Pinterest widgets, not brand UI.
- Copy, phones (303-856-8676 and alt 303-791-6480), email, hours (Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–5:00 PM MST), address, services, equipment, and gallery photos are reused. Three obvious typos in the source were corrected in the demo: “and and”, “sevices”, and “value are relationships”.
- Photos downloaded into `assets/` from the live site (logo, milling photo, catalog machine photos, flag, gallery).

## Peer sites

Layout ideas only. No text, photos, or branding copied.

### 1. Mikron Manufacturing — https://www.mikronmfg.com/

Colorado Springs precision CNC and sheet-metal shop.

Borrowed:

- Uncovered shop photograph first, with the headline and two actions (quote and capabilities) underneath rather than type sitting on the photo.
- Capabilities as a short scannable list, then a numbered path from “tell us about the job” to finished parts. The demo uses three steps built only from Pro Custom’s own sentences (idea or project, quote, professional quality parts). It does not copy Mikron’s seven-step quality system.
- A tight photo grid of real parts, labeled only as current and past work.
- Phone repeated in the header and in the closing band.

### 2. Cer-Mac Inc. — https://www.cermac.com/

Bucks County, PA precision machine shop.

Borrowed:

- Phone treated as a primary header action on larger screens, and a sticky mobile call bar here so the same idea works on a phone.
- Equipment written as a compact inventory (counts and machine types), not a paragraph and not large padded cards.
- Services grouped as small tags a buyer can scan.
- Quote form kept to the fields the shop already asks for (name, email, phone, company, preference, comments), placed on the contact page with the address beside it.
- Family-owned history stated in plain language next to the about story. Only the “over 40 years” and “family owned” lines already published by Pro Custom are used.

### 3. Owens Industries — https://www.owensind.com/

Oak Creek, WI ultra-precision CNC shop.

Borrowed:

- Dark charcoal closing band with a single strong quote button, using Pro Custom’s orange and black instead of Owens’ branding.
- Process names as compact chips (milling, turning, laser, prototypes, assembly, finishing) instead of tall marketing cards.
- Machine photography shown on a clean field with a short label under each photo (vertical machining center, turning center, fiber laser), matching photos already on their services and home pages.

Not borrowed from any peer: certifications, industry lists, tolerances, lead times, machine model specs, staff, pricing, or testimonials.
