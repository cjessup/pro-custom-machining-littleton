Our business

Pro Custom Machining is a family owned and operated business with over 40 years of experience in the machining industry.

Even in the changing market, we like to keep the "small shop" feel! We enjoy building a "face to face" relationship with every one of our customers. Customer service is our number one priority here and we strive to build long lasting relationships.

Pro Custom Machining believes in preserving manufacturing in the U.S.A. All of our products are made in house, and we value our relationships with local vendors and suppliers.
